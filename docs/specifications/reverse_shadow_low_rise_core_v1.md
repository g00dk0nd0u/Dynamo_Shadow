# Low-rise reverse-shadow core v1

## Purpose and status

`low_rise_optimized_continuous_sunlight_envelope_v2` is a pure-Python Shadow Core aid for coarse initial massing. It is not a unique or maximum legally buildable volume, is not permit certified, and does not confirm ordinance applicability. A final proposal must be checked by the existing forward equal-time shadow workflow. External specialist-software comparison has not yet been performed.

Only the low-rise calculation type is covered. High-rise reverse shadow, Revit preview/write operations, and Dynamo Player exposure are later work. Unbounded areas remain `null` rather than receiving an invented height.

## Approximation

The method deterministically selects separate near (5 m) and far (10 m) continuous sunlight intervals from all valid stepped placements, including the centered baseline and exact earliest/latest placements, to improve the coarse usable envelope. This remains a conservative continuous-sunlight sufficient condition; it is not exact cumulative-duration inverse optimization. The measurement lines are approximate signed-distance contours generated by Marching Squares on a 1 m site-only grid; they are not exact statutory polygon offsets. Points are resampled along each closed line.

| Profile | Site-distance grid | Measurement spacing | Height-field XY | Sun step | Vertical step |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Rough | 1 m | 4 m | 4 m | 30 minutes | 0.5 m |
| Standard | 1 m | 1 m | 1 m | 15 minutes | 0.5 m |
| High | 1 m | 1 m | 1 m | 15 minutes | 0.5 m |

Reverse resolution is independent by purpose. The site-distance grid defaults to 1 m; measurement spacing, height-field XY spacing, vertical height step, and sun step are preset-dependent. A 1 m XY height field is formally supported and a future 0.5 m XY profile is not prohibited. Standard and High currently use 1 m site-distance, measurement, and height-field spacing, 15-minute sun steps, and conservative 0.5 m vertical flooring. Rough retains its lightweight 1 m / 4 m / 4 m / 30-minute settings. There is no automatic accuracy fallback.

Adjacent sun rays define planar facets; the solver does not linearly interpolate `tan(altitude)` by azimuth. Each facet height is conservatively clamped to the lower endpoint-altitude slope to suppress over-height between coarse time samples. This planning convention is not a certified legal safety margin.

## Height field and mesh

The deterministic row-major field records site classification, nullable raw analytical and conservative 0.5 m-quantized bounded heights, governing zone, measurement point, facet, times, actual horizontal distance, and the 5 m/10 m measurement-line distance. The JSON-safe top-surface mesh references field grid indices rather than duplicating vertices. A fully inside, fully bounded cell becomes two triangles on one fixed diagonal only when no site-boundary segment crosses its interior. Partial-boundary and unbounded cells are omitted. Oriented triangle boundary edges are stitched into deterministic closed index loops for future side-face generation. Plan area and volume are coarse reference summaries, not statutory maxima. Empty bounded fields, empty meshes, and invalid candidate volumes are failures rather than complete results.

Candidate selection uses a single-process, two-pass design. Pass 1 evaluates row chunks with compact `array('d')` buffers and a NaN sentinel, retaining only per-pair score aggregates; Pass 2 recomputes only the selected near/far pair and builds metadata and mesh output. Fixed guards stop oversized site grids, height grids, measurement sets, constraint checks, and meshes. They never coarsen the requested profile automatically. All certification flags remain `false`, including `permit_ready_certified=false`.

## Standard API audit

Revit native geometry and Dynamo geometry nodes were considered but are not applicable to this intentionally pure-Python, JSON-safe calculation core. Existing repository signed-distance, point-in-polygon, Marching Squares, and NOAA solar facilities are reused. Revit 2024.3 runtime verification and `TessellatedShapeBuilder` conversion belong to the subsequent preview work.
