# Calculation accuracy presets

Dynamo Player exposes Forward Fast (`rough`, 1.0 m / 30 minutes), Standard (`standard`, 0.5 m / 15 minutes), and High (`high`, 0.25 m / 5 minutes). Fast is for rapid initial iteration, Standard is for normal design and remains the Player default, and High is for final high-precision checks with increased runtime. The legacy internal ids remain stable. A supplied Player preset overrides the corresponding settings JSON values; when the Player input is absent, the existing pure-Python diagnostic defaults remain unchanged. Accuracy is never reduced automatically when a safety limit is exceeded.

Reverse accuracy remains independently defined. Reverse Fast and Standard are supported; Reverse High currently retains the existing Reverse Standard-level values and must not be interpreted as Forward High accuracy. Forward calculation sample density does not control preview density: preview continues to select configured reference/hourly slices rather than creating a DirectShape for every internal sample. High continues to use the sparse-tile and compact-large duration paths and does not fall back automatically to a lower-accuracy preset.

The duration grid analysis bounds are not clipped to the site boundary. The current calculation includes the bounds of every unified formal shadow polygon and then applies the analysis safety margin. When site-boundary geometry becomes available, the contract is the union of all unified shadow polygon bounds and the site-boundary bounds expanded by at least 10 m, followed by the analysis safety margin. The site boundary is a future legal-evaluation mask, not a duration-grid clipping boundary; this change does not implement site-boundary geometry processing.

Equal-time contours remain the original Marching Squares polylines from the duration grid. No Chaikin, spline/NURBS, moving-average, vertex-displacement, or display-only smoothing is applied.
